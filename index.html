MY ENDING TO MY BOOK OF LIFE

My memories.

DECEMBER 2, 2008

The day I was born.

Two people from completely different lives had somehow ended up together.

One came from a poor village, but from a family that was happy.

The other came from a rich family with a broken marriage.

And then...

"Oh. A male baby."

Three daughters had come before me.

CHAPTER I

BEFORE I KNEW

Before school, I was raised as someone who was expected to always be useful.

"Ryo, Ryo, can you get that?"

"Ryo, that's too heavy."

"It's fine. Do it for me."

And more.

People called it a happy family.
A rich family.
A lucky family.

Lucky to have three nice older sisters, they said.

I believed them.

CHAPTER II

FAMILY

My parents were too busy managing their company.

Coming home sometimes felt like I was interrupting their work.

Then there were my three older sisters.

In my eyes, they were everything a younger brother could ask for.

Smart.
Athletic.
Funny.
Talented.

Everything.

And then...

Strict.
Cruel.
Cold.
Hypocritical.

Haaa...

Maybe this is what they mean when they say:

Don't judge a family before looking inside it.

"Rich family."
"Nepo baby."
"Born lucky."

CHAPTER III

SCHOOL LIFE

I was one of the top students in my class.

Always close to the top.
Never quite number one.

For four years, I became the person people came to when they needed someone to listen.

A school therapist.
Three awards.

I was athletic too.

A closet full of trophies.

Proof that I could do something right.

But apparently...

I wasn't good enough for first place.

"Disappointment."

A therapist who could help people but couldn't figure out how to help himself.

I was pushed into being athletic because I wanted my father's approval.

I wanted more friends.

But trust became difficult.
Attachment became difficult.

And keeping myself together became even harder.

CHAPTER IV

ANOTHER WORLD

Somewhere around thirteen or fourteen, I discovered the internet.

I didn't have a phone.

But I had a computer.

Two years of gaming.

Two years of finding another world outside my own.

Then, at fifteen, I was given an old phone.

A hand-me-down.

Strict parental controls.
A curfew.
8 PM until 7 AM.

And then...

Discord.

I found friends.

A group that started around 2021 and somehow lasted until now.

I miss them.

They grew up before I did.

Life kept moving.

THE PERSON

SOMETHING I FOUND

Dating in real life was never really allowed for me.

I tried.

I got punished for it.

So the internet became my chance.

To me, dating was never about finding some perfect person.

I only wanted someone who would love me and understand me.

Maybe someone like that didn't exist.

Or maybe they were simply too far out of reach.

I didn't give up.

I had too much hope in humanity.

I was tossed aside more times than I want to remember.

And then I joined a matchmaking server.

I found friends.

I found someone.


MY LOVE

FEBRUARY 2, 2026

HER

My memories are fuzzy now.

But I remember meeting her.

The best person I had ever met.

Twice as strong as me.
Funny.
Loving.
Strong.

And most importantly...

She understood people.

She made me regain hope.

For a while, life was happy.

My sisters even loved the new obedient me.

I was happy.

No matter how many commands they threw at me, I was happy.

For once, I thought I had found something worth holding onto.

ARCHIVE

THE PART I DON'T TALK ABOUT

Before the internet, life was already difficult.

Not because we were poor.

We weren't.

We lived in a villa.

Five cars.

Eventually, they didn't even fit through the gates anymore.

Money was never really the problem.

My family was.

There were things behind those walls that nobody outside the family knew about.

Being hit was called a "loving lesson."

Being controlled was called discipline.

Being hurt was something I was expected to forgive.

Someone I was supposed to trust crossed a boundary that should never have been crossed.

i was sexually assaulted by my own flesh and blood

I was scared to tell anyone.pictures were taken.

I stayed quiet.

3 "suicide." attempts failed.

uncountble amount of "self harm" is done and still doing now

I carried it alone for longer than I should have.

Everyone else could still look at us and see a normal family.

"Rich family."
"Nepo baby."
"Born lucky."

They saw the villa.
They saw the cars.
They saw the money.

They never saw what happened when nobody else was looking.

CHAPTER V

LEAVING

Eventually, I moved far away from my family.

The excuse was simple:

Taking care of Mom's other branch of the company in Malaysia.

My sisters were furious.

They took away my allowance.

So I survived on my own paycheck.

My own house.
My own food.
My own vehicle.

My own life.

It was tiring.

Really tiring.

But it was mine.

2026

And now...

University is coming.

A new chapter.

A new place.

A new life.

Everyone keeps saying I'm moving forward.

Maybe I am.

But the more the days passed, the less I felt like myself.

I started looking at myself and wondering who I had become.

BEFORE I GO

THANK YOU

Thank you to every person, every place, every moment, every mistake, and every stupid little thing that brought me here.

The people who stayed.

The people who left.

The people I met for only a short part of my life.

The friends from school.

The friends from the internet.

The people who made me laugh when I didn't feel like laughing.

All of you became part of the story.
